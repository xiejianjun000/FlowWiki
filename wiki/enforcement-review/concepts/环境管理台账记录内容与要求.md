# 环境管理台账记录内容与要求

Environmental management records are a key component of the permit system. Five categories: 1) Basic info; 2) Production facility operation; 3) Pollution control facility operation; 4) Monitoring records; 5) Other EHS info. Records can be electronic + paper, retained >= 5 years.
