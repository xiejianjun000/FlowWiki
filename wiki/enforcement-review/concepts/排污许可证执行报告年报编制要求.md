# 排污许可证执行报告年报编制要求

《Paiwu Xuke Guanli Tiaoli》Article 22: permit holders shall submit annual compliance reports by end of January (thermal power: Jan 15). Steel/cement/paper industries (from 2025) use unified reporting. Penalty for non-submission: RMB 5,000-20,000 per violation (Article 37).
